# Prompt templates

Fill the brackets, delete what you do not need. Pair with `instructions.md` for the rules behind them.

**Single move**

> [shot size], [angle / height], [lens or depth of field].
> [subject] is [action]; the camera does a [move], from [start position / subject] moving [direction] at [speed], following or circling [object].
> It starts on [start size / framing] and stops on [end size / framing] when [event] happens.
> The image stays [smooth / lightly handheld]; [distance / subject position / focal length / focus] holds throughout.

**Follow with constant subject size**

> [shot size]; the camera follows [subject] from [front / behind / side].
> It holds [distance] and matches their speed, so their size and position in frame stay steady.
> Natural background parallax; one continuous move, no change of camera position.

**Reveal**

> The shot starts on [known information] and moves by [pan / tilt / truck / pedestal / pull out], [direction and speed].
> On the way it shows [middle information] in order, and stops on [key information].
> The key information stays hidden until the end; once it's reached, the camera settles and holds.

**Handheld presence**

> [shot size and angle], [restrained / documentary / violent] handheld.
> The camera follows [subject's action] from [direction], keeping [distance and framing].
> The image keeps [slight breathing drift / footstep bob / obvious chasing shake]; the subject stays readable.
> No [random shake / sudden zoom / accidental overtaking].

**Continuous gimbal follow**

> Single continuous gimbal follow.
> The camera follows [subject] from [front / behind / side] through [waypoint 1] and [waypoint 2];
> after [trigger], it moves smoothly from [old position] to [new position].
> [subject size / distance / focus] holds throughout; smooth, with natural inertia, no cut.

**FPV route**

> First-person FPV shot, starting at [start point and height], flying [direction] at [speed];
> first [through / around waypoint 1], then [climb / dive / turn] past [waypoint 2];
> finally slows and stops at [end point].
> Maximum bank about [angle]; no passing through objects, no full roll, no cuts.

**Dolly zoom**

> A [symmetrical / deep] [location], the subject centred.
> The camera slowly [pushes in / pulls out] while the lens zooms the opposite way [out / in],
> keeping the subject's [head and shoulders / full body] the same size and position in frame,
> so the background perspective keeps [stretching / compressing]. Continuous, no orbit, the pose doesn't change.

**Long take and combined move**

> Single continuous shot, no cuts.
> Stage 1: the camera starts at [start point] and uses [main move] to follow [subject] to [waypoint];
> when [trigger] happens, stage 2 switches to [secondary move] and reveals [new information];
> it ends on [final size and framing].
> Identity, direction and light stay continuous; no teleporting, no sudden change of camera position.

**Choreography (main + secondary + trigger + holds)**

> Main move: [the most important path]
> Secondary move: [the second action, only at one point]
> Trigger: [subject action or place in space]
> Holds: [subject size, eye line, focus, axis, continuity]
